# MalumeScholarTrack — Seedance Video Prompts (Higgsfield)

## Brand tokens
- Colours: zinc-950 bg (#0A0A0B), amber-500/600 (#F59E0B/#D97706), light amber dash (#FCD34D)
- Logo: bold amber "M" with road-dash baseline on near-black rounded square
- Tone: warm, trustworthy, South African, not corporate

---

## 1. Logo reveal (5s) — app launch / intro stinger

**Prompt:**
Near-black background. A bold amber letter M draws itself on screen with a glowing road-dash line underneath, like a road being paved in real time. The M strokes animate with a smooth draw-on effect, amber gradient shimmer. Camera: locked, centered, no movement. Lighting: subtle amber glow from below. Style: premium motion graphics, minimal, clean, no clutter. 4K. Duration 5 seconds. No text, no people.

**Negative:** no characters, no faces, no text, no clutter, no lens flare, no purple, no blue.

---

## 2. Parent trust (15s) — Facebook/Instagram ads

**Prompt:**
Cinematic handheld shot inside a warm South African family home at dawn. A mother in school uniform prep hands her daughter a backpack. Cut to: phone screen close-up showing a live map with an amber bus icon moving along a route, driver photo and green "verified" badge visible. Cut to: the daughter climbing into a clean scholar-transport vehicle, driver in branded shirt gives a nod. Cut back to: mother at the window, phone in hand, relieved smile. Colour grade: warm amber tones over cool morning light. Camera: shallow depth of field, 35mm, natural handheld. Duration 15 seconds. Mood: safe, warm, trusted.

**Negative:** no stock-footage look, no American accents, no suburban American houses, no corporate office.

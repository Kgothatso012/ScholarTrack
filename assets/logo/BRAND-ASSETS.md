# MalumeScholarTrack — Brand Assets

## Marketing Logo (use this everywhere external)
**File:** `marketing-logo.jpg` (or `-hd` / `-md` / `-sm` for sizes)
- Illustrated style: minibus + Union Buildings + three pins + full wordmark
- Colours: navy `#1E3A5F`, orange `#E8871A`, green `#2E8B57`, cream bg
- Use for: social posts, ads, flyers, website hero, WhatsApp profile pic, Google Play feature graphic, email headers
- Square crop: `marketing-logo-square-{512,1024}.jpg` for profile pics / app store icons

## App Icon (in-app, launcher)
**File:** `../icon.png` (1024×1024)
- Simple amber "M" with road-dash baseline on near-black
- Use for: app launcher, notification icon, favicon — anything tiny where the illustrated logo turns to mush
- Do NOT use the illustrated marketing logo as the launcher icon — it won't read at 48px

## Splash Screen
**File:** `../splash-icon.png` (1284×1284, zinc-950 bg)
- Uses the M mark, not the illustrated logo (keeps splash clean and fast)

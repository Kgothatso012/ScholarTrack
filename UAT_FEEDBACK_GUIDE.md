# MalumeScholarTrack UAT Feedback Form

## 📋 Google Form Setup Guide

### Questions for ALL Testers:

1. **Name** (optional text)
2. **Phone Number** (optional text)
3. **Role Tested** (dropdown: Parent / Driver / Admin)
4. **Did you successfully login?** (dropdown: Yes / No)
5. **What device are you using?** (dropdown: Android / iPhone / Other)
6. **Screenshot** (file upload - optional)

---

### Questions for PARENTS:

7. **Could you add your child?** (Yes / Partially / No)
8. **Could you hire a driver?** (Yes / No - if no, explain)
9. **Can you see live location?** (Yes / No)
10. **Rate ease of use** (1-5 stars)
11. **What's missing?** (long text)

---

### Questions for DRIVERS:

7. **Can you toggle availability?** (Yes / No)
8. **Can you upload compliance docs?** (Yes / No)
9. **Does earnings show correctly?** (Yes / No)
10. **Rate ease of use** (1-5 stars)
11. **What's missing?** (long text)

---

### Bug Report Section:

12. **Device Model** (text)
13. **Android/iOS Version** (text)
14. **Steps to reproduce** (long text)
15. **What happened** (long text)
16. **Screenshot** (file upload)

---

### Create it at:
**https://forms.google.com**

Click "Forms" → "New Form" → Paste these questions!

## Full Description (4000 chars)

MalumeScholarTrack connects parents, verified drivers, and schools to keep children safe during school transport. Built for South African parents who want peace of mind — every trip tracked, every child accounted for.

**For Parents:**
• Real-time bus tracking — see your child's location during pickup and drop-off
• Driver verification — every driver is background-checked with valid PDP, license, and insurance
• Emergency SOS — one-tap panic alert with live location to your phone
• Push notifications — instant alerts when your child is picked up, dropped off, or arrives at school
• Trip history — review all past trips, routes, and driver ratings
• Link children to routes — assign your children to specific drivers and routes

**For Drivers:**
• Trip management — accept and manage parent requests
• Vehicle checklists — complete safety inspections before each trip
• Compliance documents — upload and manage PDP, license, insurance, roadworthy certificates
• Real-time location sharing — parents see your live route during trips
• Driver ratings — earn trust through consistent, safe service

**For Schools:**
• Dashboard view — monitor all active trips in your area
• Attendance tracking — confirm which children arrived safely

**Why South African parents trust MalumeScholarTrack:**
• All drivers are verified against South African regulations (PDP, NHBRC standards)
• Built-in emergency response — SOS alerts go directly to parents with live GPS
• Offline support — the app works even with poor connectivity
• Privacy-first design — your child's data is protected under POPIA

MalumeScholarTrack is developed by Makena Building Group (Pty) Ltd, registered in Pretoria.

## Data Safety (Google Play required since May 2022)

| Category | Data Collected | How Used |
|----------|---------------|----------|
| Personal Info | Name, email, phone number | Account creation, driver/parent matching |
| Location | Precise GPS location | Live tracking during trips, geofencing |
| Financial Info | Payment processing via Paystack | Trip payments |
| Contacts | Phone number | Emergency contacts |
| Device Info | Device type, OS version | App performance optimization |

**Does this app share data with third parties?**
Yes — payment data is shared with Paystack for transaction processing. Location data is shared with linked parents/drivers during active trips only.

**Is data encrypted in transit?**
Yes — all communication uses TLS 1.2+ encryption.

**Can users request data deletion?**
Yes — email hello@malumescholartrack.co.za or use the in-app account deletion feature.
